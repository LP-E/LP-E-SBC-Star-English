# LP-E-SBC-Star

Strong and future-minded project.

Its strong with its RK3588s chip , built-in sensors , ethernet support (up to 1000 gigabit) , Fast experience with the HDMI and MiniHDMI port* , and power to your DIY projects with the 40 Pin GPIO and more!

## Features:
- Octa-core 4x Cortex A76 and 4x Cortex A55 (2.4 Ghz and 1.8 Ghz)
- 8nm LP
- ARM Mali-G610 MP4 (Vulkan 1.2, OpenGL ES 3.2, OpenCL 2.2)
- 8K@60fps**
- HDMI , miniHDMI ve MIPI-DSI
- For the camera :  48MP ISP ve MIPI-CSI
- 4GB LPDDR4X SDRAM
- 2x USB3.1 , 2x USB 2.0 , 40 pin GPIO is available.
- 6 TOPS
